Featured Research

SIGGRAPH 2026 · Poster & Interactive VR Demo · 2025 — Present

Gesture-Controlled Quantum Information in Virtual Reality

A Unity-based VR system that makes quantum states graspable. Users place quantum gates — H, X, Y, Z, CNOT — onto a spatial circuit with their bare hands and watch single- and two-qubit states evolve as real-time Bloch sphere visualizations with live measurement probabilities. Gesture triggers, spatial targets, and gaze guidance turn abstract linear algebra into embodied, explorable interaction.

Role: interaction design, Unity (C#) development, hand-tracking implementation, spatial guidance and visual feedback design, system integration, prototype testing.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Meta Quest 3
  • XR Interaction Toolkit
  • Hand Tracking
  • Qiskit
  • OpenQASM 2.0
  • Bloch Sphere Visualization

SIGGRAPH 2024 · Immersive Pavilion · 2023–2024

WaterForm — Multisensory Liquid Haptic System

A liquid-based haptic system that expands how water is perceived in VR. Eight water forms — splash, flow, gravity, wind, resistance, buoyancy, mechanical energy, and mist — delivered through a 245 × 245 cm pool installation with a four-pump system, synchronized with the VR scene.

Role: hardware fabrication, interactive programming, system integration.

SIGGRAPH Asia 2024 · XR Program · 2024

Dragravity — Dynamic Weight-Transfer Haptic Device

A wearable, reconfigurable haptic device that creates full-body weight sensations in VR. Movable water bags and elastic tension transfer weight across the body, letting users feel sinking, pressure, gravity shifts, and upward pull.

Role: hardware fabrication, interactive programming, system integration.

Empathy Wearable · 2023–2024

The Awake: GravityCoat

A wearable that simulates the bodily weight and movement limits of ALS patients through water-weight distribution — up to 5.5 kg pumped across the garment as the wearer sinks, lies trapped, and resurfaces in a VR narrative.

Role: hardware fabrication, interactive programming, system integration.
